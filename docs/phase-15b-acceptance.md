## Browser evidence and limitations

- Local HTTPS in-app browser: English desktop registration had labeled inputs; Arabic RTL and English LTR registration were inspected at 450 px with no horizontal overflow. A disposable `example.test` learner registered, signed in/out; dashboard showed Student role and only two catalog-view permissions. Direct `/admin` initially exposed category/course counts because those view permissions were shared. The new API/SPA boundary now requires a staff role or a specifically granted non-catalog backoffice permission; the learner retest displayed 403. Existing direct-permission authorization remains supported and each action policy still applies. Feature and Vue regression tests cover this boundary.
- Known disposable email and unknown email returned identical neutral forgot-password copy. Local `MAIL_MAILER=log` means **no real delivery**. Valid-link reset, throttling and two-device invalidation were not browser-accepted.
- English privacy, terms and refund routes showed the unpublished notice. Arabic refund did too; at 450 px the document reported `lang=ar`, `dir=rtl`, no horizontal overflow. Admin draft/publish was not browser-accepted. No legal copy was invented or published.
- A local demo Admin account allowed read-only inspection of `/admin/courses/3/edit`: certificate enablement, lesson percentage, final quiz, required assignments, Admin approval and save controls rendered, along with an unconfigured-policy warning. No existing course setting was changed. This confirms the settings surface loads, **not** policy save, approval, issuance, PDF or QR acceptance. No Content Manager/Sales Support browser credentials or staging deployment were supplied for real multipart, purchase, Support, Assignment, approval, QR/PDF scan, or full accessibility/device testing. Automated/mocked tests cannot close those rows.

## Environment and invariant findings

- Current reset rotates remember token, deletes personal-access tokens and deletes the user's session rows **only** with `session.driver=database` (the current local setting). Another session store needs equivalent invalidation and a real two-session test.
- Lesson video stores `video_url`; Vue places it directly in `<video src>`. Lesson API guards protect metadata/page, not necessarily video bytes. Protected, expiring delivery or explicit launch-scope risk decision is required before selling paid video.
- No verified database+private-file backup schedule, retention, separate restore rehearsal, recovery objective or alerting evidence was supplied. An existing backup without restore evidence is insufficient.
- Checked local config without secrets: `APP_ENV=local`, `APP_DEBUG=true`, HTTPS app URL, database session/cache/queue, log mailer, local filesystem and USD commerce currency. These are not production readiness.
- Before migration, local DB had three certificate-enabled courses and one issued certificate. Three additive certificate migrations were applied locally; historical certificate rows were untouched. All three courses need explicit policy setup before **new** issuance. Local role seeder was not rerun.
